About the Team

Penta Minds is built by emerging researchers who contribute across research, operations, outreach, digital systems, and other areas essential to building a sustained research ecosystem.

Our team brings together different perspectives, skills, areas of interest, and domains of expertise around a shared commitment to meaningful research and responsible work. Members contribute through defined roles and functions while working collaboratively across divisions, research programmes, and organisational initiatives.

The Team Division brings together the people who contribute to Penta Minds, the roles they undertake, the departments they support, and the opportunities through which others can become part of the organisation.

The Divisions We Operate

Research Division

Leads Penta Minds’ research activities, including research design, field studies, data work, analysis, documentation, and scholarly outcomes.

Outreach Division

Builds and maintains relationships with institutions, communities, and external partners while coordinating outreach, field engagement, and research participation.

Advisory Committee

Provides strategic, academic, and domain-level guidance to support research quality, institutional development, and long-term direction.

Security Operations

Supports the security, privacy, and operational resilience of Penta Minds’ digital infrastructure, information, systems, and organisational processes.

Digital Operations

Manages the digital infrastructure and platforms that support Penta Minds, including websites, digital systems, communications platforms, and online operations.

Our Operational Team

The active researchers and contributors currently driving Penta Minds as an independent research initiative.

Associates

Supporting contributors assisting with field visits, survey distribution, and community outreach across the research ecosystem.

Join the Team

Penta Minds brings together students, emerging researchers, and contributors with different skills and interests to build a sustained research ecosystem. Explore the contribution divisions below to find where your skills, interests, and capacity can contribute.

Research Contributors

Contribute across the research lifecycle, from design and literature to field, analysis, documentation, publications, and the development of resources.

Areas of contribution: Research · Data & Analysis · Field Research · Documentation · Methodology · Publications · Resources · Knowledge Management

Outreach & Partnerships

Build connections with students, educators, institutions, communities, and external partners while supporting research outreach, institutional engagement, partnerships, and collaborative initiatives.

Areas of contribution: Outreach · Institutional Relations · Partnerships · Field Coordination · Community Engagement

Digital & Security Operations

Build, maintain, and protect the digital infrastructure, platforms, systems, and online presence that support Penta Minds and its research ecosystem.

Areas of contribution: Backend & Web · Digital Infrastructure · Platform Operations · Systems Administration · Information Security · Privacy & Access Management · Security Operations

Operations & Management

Support the internal functioning, coordination, administration, and management of Penta Minds while helping teams and programmes translate objectives into execution.

Areas of contribution: Team Coordination · Administration · Operations · Programme Management · Division Management · Project Coordination · Resource Management

Community & Management

Help build and sustain the Penta Minds and community by supporting members, internal engagement, knowledge sharing, participation, and community initiatives.

Areas of contribution: Member Engagement · Community Management · Internal Communications & Co-Ordination · Events & Activities · Membership Support
