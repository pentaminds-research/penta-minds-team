Penta Minds

A student-led research collective organised into divisions. Every member contributes to an ecosystem built for rigorous, accessible, and collaborative AI research.

Meet the Team Join the Team

About the Team

Penta Minds operates as a modular research team. Each member contributes specific domain knowledge and field capacity. The team is organised not by hierarchy of seniority, but by function: research leads, field contributors, and outreach associates working in parallel toward shared research outputs.
